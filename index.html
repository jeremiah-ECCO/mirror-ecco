The work of the Mirror.

Some patterns only become visible when they are reflected back. Most lives carry a through-line that the person living them cannot see — too close to the work, too inside the days, too good at minimizing what they've actually built.

The Infinity Mirror is the secular, professional instantiation of an old human gesture: to be witnessed accurately by someone who has no reason to flatter you. An astrologer's reading. A spiritual director's attention. A trusted elder's careful naming. We bring the same care to the question of what someone's life has actually been about — and we deliver it as an artifact they can keep.

This is not coaching. There is no recurring arrangement. There is one page, built with attention, delivered once, archived permanently. What they do with it is theirs.

— Jeremiah W. Hearne, ECCO
